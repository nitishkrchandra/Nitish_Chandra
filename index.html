Quantum computing · Error correction · Networks

Reliable quantum computing, at scale.

I am a Ph.D. Candidate at the University of Pittsburgh, advised by Prof. Kaushik P. Seshadreesan. My research focuses on scalable fault-tolerant quantum computing with an emphasis on error correction, distributed architectures, and architecture-aware compilation.

I study how logical quantum operations can be implemented across networked quantum processors and how communication constraints affect the reliability and resource cost of fault-tolerant systems.
