Distributed fault-tolerant quantum computing

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in the Department of Informatics and Networked Systems at the University of Pittsburgh, advised by Prof. Kaushik P. Seshadreesan.

My research focuses on quantum error correction, fault-tolerant logical operations, and architecture-aware compilation for networked quantum processors.
