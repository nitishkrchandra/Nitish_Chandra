About

About me

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in the Department of Informatics and Networked Systems at the University of Pittsburgh, advised by Prof. Kaushik P. Seshadreesan.

My research focuses on distributed fault-tolerant quantum computing, quantum error correction, and architecture-aware compilation. I study how quantum processors can work together reliably despite communication noise and limited network resources.

In 2026, I was a Software Engineer Ph.D. Intern at Cisco Quantum Lab, working on logical computation and compilation for modular quantum architectures.

Research interests

  • Distributed quantum error correction. Color codes and qLDPC codes on modular architectures with noisy interconnects.
  • Fault-tolerant logical operations. Distributed lattice surgery and logical operations across networked processors.
  • Network-aware compilation and scheduling. Mapping quantum workloads under communication, latency, and resource constraints.
